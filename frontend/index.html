How it works

Three steps to a new emotional state.

01
Set your coordinates. Click on the emotional plane to mark where you are now, then where you want to be.
02
The engine plots a path. Choose a trajectory mode — direct, curved, or exploratory. Ascend generates waypoints across Valence-Arousal space.
03
Tracks are matched by feeling. Each waypoint finds the closest song in emotional space. Listen, skip, or reshape — the path bends to you.
▲ ASCEND
Why it’s different

Psychology, not tags.

Most music recommendation relies on genre labels or collaborative filtering. Ascend is rooted in the circumplex model of affect — every track is scored on two continuous dimensions: Valence (pleasure) and Arousal (intensity).

  • No mood labels. No “Chill Vibes.” No “Happy Hits.”
  • No genre walls. A classical piece and an electronic track can sit side by side if their emotional signatures match.
  • Adaptive feedback. Each session tunes the system to your unique perception.
